<objective>
Close D-12: turn the 11 WoW class colours and 4 role colours from raw hex constants into paired
light/dark CSS custom properties, tuned so every class keeps its recognisable hue while staying
legible on the new light surface — the "Wowhead approach" — and repoint every DOM consumer at the
token while preserving the one legitimate raw-hex exception.

Purpose: these values are consumed through inline `style={{ color }}`, not through `className`, so
they cannot be fixed by the Tailwind-class sweep in plan 01-06. They need their own remediation, and
they are the single largest light-mode legibility risk in the analysis tables: Priest ships as pure
white and Rogue as near-yellow.

Output: `--class-*` / `--role-*` token pairs, a `lib/constants.ts` split into token maps and
Satori-only hex maps, and nine consumer components reading through helpers.
</objective>
