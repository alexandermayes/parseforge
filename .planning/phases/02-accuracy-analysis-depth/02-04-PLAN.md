## Phase Goal

Raiders get verifiably correct analysis plus the per-fight depth competitors already offer.

<objective>
Stop judging healers with a damage dealer's yardstick. A healer viewing their own row today sees a
single HPS number and a percentile; after this plan they see effective HPS, overheal percent and
healing uptime, each sitting beside the same figure for the top-ranked healers of their spec.

The plan leads with a tracer through the player-page path — shared helper, top-player fetch, analyze
route, comparison card — then makes the raid overview's Healer Breakdown panel consume the identical
helper and the identical colour bands, which is what makes D-08's guarantee real: one computation, two
surfaces, no possible disagreement.

Purpose: close ACC-04's success criterion — "a healer viewing their own row sees analysis that reflects
healing decisions, not a DPS-shaped ranking" (D-05, D-06, D-08).
Output: `lib/healer-metrics.ts` as the single source of truth, top-healer comparison values on the
analyze payload, two new comparison rows on the healer card, and a Healer Breakdown panel reading the
same numbers through the same helpers.
</objective>
