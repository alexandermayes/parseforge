## Phase Goal

Raiders get verifiably correct analysis plus the per-fight depth competitors already offer.

<objective>
Finish ACC-04 by changing what a healer is *told*, not just what they are shown. Plan 02-04 put effective
HPS, overheal percent and healing uptime on the page; today the advice beneath those numbers is still
damage-dealer advice — the same "keep your global cooldown rolling" rule and casts-per-minute comparison
that `generateSuggestions` applies to both roles.

This plan adds three healer rules driven by the new metrics, each thresholded against the top healers'
own values rather than a magic constant, and stops the DPS-shaped rules from reaching a healer at all.

Purpose: close D-07 so a healer's suggestions reflect healing decisions.
Output: role-gated suggestion rules with tests, a category label for them, and healer props on the
analyze-completion event.
</objective>

Express every threshold as a ratio against the top value, never as an absolute percentage:

Overheal: fire when the player's overheal percent exceeds the top healers' by a factor you declare as a

Uptime: fire when the player's uptime percent falls below the top healers' by a declared factor — 0.9 is a
defensible starting multiplier, meaning roughly a tenth less time spent actively healing. Priority high when
the shortfall is large, medium otherwise.

the overheal and the uptime rules did not fire, because that combination is what distinguishes a gear or
consumables problem from a technique problem. Use the existing `dps.gapToMedian` and `dps.gapToTop` figures
for the percentage rather than recomputing a gap — they are already the numbers the card displays, and a
suggestion that quotes a different number than the card above it undermines both.

figure beside it misinforms: high overheal can be correct play, and only the comparison makes it a signal.
